# 🚗 Electric Vehicle Data Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing Electric Vehicle (EV) data to understand market trends, vehicle distribution, and adoption patterns. The dashboard provides insights into EV growth, top manufacturers, and regional performance using interactive visualizations.

---

## 🎯 Objective
- Analyze overall EV market size and growth
- Compare Battery Electric Vehicles (BEV) and Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEV)
- Identify top manufacturers and models
- Understand geographic distribution of EVs
- Evaluate impact of CAFV (Clean Alternative Fuel Vehicle) eligibility

---

## 📊 Key KPIs
- Total Vehicles (BEV + PHEV)
- Average Electric Range
- Total BEV Vehicles and %
- Total PHEV Vehicles and %

---

## 📈 Visualizations
- 📅 **EV Growth Over Time** (Line Chart)
- 🌍 **Vehicles by State** (Map Chart)
- 🏭 **Top 10 Manufacturers** (Bar Chart)
- 🚘 **Top 10 Models** (Tree Map)
- 🥧 **CAFV Eligibility Distribution** (Pie/Donut Chart)

---

## 🔍 Key Insights
- EV adoption has increased significantly over the years
- BEVs dominate the market compared to PHEVs
- Certain manufacturers lead the EV industry
- Some regions show higher EV adoption due to infrastructure and policies
- CAFV eligibility impacts vehicle adoption

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel (Data Cleaning)
- Power BI / Tableau (Dashboard & Visualization)

---

## 📂 Dataset
- EV dataset containing vehicle type, model, make, year, state, electric range, and CAFV eligibility

---

## 🚀 How to Use
1. Download the project files
2. Open the dashboard in Power BI / Tableau
3. Interact with filters and visuals to explore insights

---

## 📌 Conclusion
This project demonstrates how raw EV data can be transformed into meaningful insights using data visualization tools, helping stakeholders make data-driven decisions.
